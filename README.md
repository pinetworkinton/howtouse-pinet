# howtouse-pinet
How to Use Smart Contracts on the Pinet Platform - Step-by-Step

1. Introduction

"Hello everyone! In this video, we'll walk you through how to use the smart contracts on the Pinet platform. Pinet is a decentralized marketplace for green energy trading, and we use smart contracts to facilitate energy transactions. In this video, we'll cover the steps for creating a shipment, approving it, confirming the shipment, and the final 'attest' step. Stay tuned!"

2. Step 1: Create Shipment

"The first step to start an energy transaction on Pinet is to create a shipment. This is done using the createShipment smart contract function. Here, you will provide details about the energy produced, such as the amount of energy, price, and producer information. This information is then sent to the blockchain to begin the transaction."

3. Step 2: Approve Payment

"After creating the shipment, the next step is to approve the payment. This is where the consumer gives permission to pay the producer for the energy. Using the approve function, the consumer authorizes the amount for the energy shipment. This step is essential to ensure both parties have agreed to the transaction."

4. Step 3: Confirm Shipment

"After payment approval, the next step is confirming the shipment. The producer confirms that the energy has been successfully delivered to the consumer. Once confirmed, the energy is transferred to the consumer, and the contract is finalized."

5. Step 4: Attest Shipment

"Finally, the last step is the 'Attest' function, where an admin verifies the entire process. This step ensures that the transaction has been executed correctly and is stored on the blockchain as proof of completion."
